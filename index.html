AI in manufacturing at Kelvion

Manufacturers have to decide what to build, buy and staff months before orders arrive. AI helps close that gap by learning from sales signals and history to predict demand, so plants can plan capacity and materials earlier.

At Kelvion, a global heat exchanger manufacturer, I work on exactly this in the SIOP (Sales, Inventory & Operations Planning) team. I build AI-driven order intake forecasting: machine learning models that combine CRM sales-pipeline signals with ERP order history to predict incoming demand per site. The forecasts feed the monthly planning cycle used by teams across several countries.

Around the models, I automate the data flow end to end, so planners get fresh, reliable predictions without manual preparation. Kelvion's heat exchangers keep machines cool; my job is to keep the planners cool when the orders come in.
